## Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) Analysis

### LTV Calculation Methodology

**LTV = ARPU × Gross Margin % × Average Customer Lifetime (months) / (1 + Discount Rate)^n**

### Assumptions

- **Gross Margin:** 75% (SaaS standard)
- **Average Customer Lifetime:** 36 months (3 years)
- **Churn Rate:** 5% monthly (95% retention)
- **Discount Rate:** 10% (cost of capital)
- **Expansion Revenue:** 8% annual increase (upsells to higher tiers)

### LTV Calculation

**Base LTV (no expansion):**
- ARPU: R 4,599/month (Year 2 average)
- Gross Margin: 75%
- Lifetime: 36 months
- LTV = R 4,599 × 0.75 × 36 = R 124,173

**LTV with Expansion (8% annual upsell):**
- Year 1: R 4,349 × 0.75 × 12 = R 39,141
- Year 2: R 4,349 × 1.08 × 0.75 × 12 = R 42,292
- Year 3: R 4,349 × 1.08² × 0.75 × 12 = R 45,676
- Total LTV = R 127,109

**LTV with Churn Adjustment (5% monthly):**
- Effective lifetime: 20 months (not 36)
- Adjusted LTV = R 127,109 × (20/36) = R 70,616

**Conservative LTV Estimate:** R 70,000-90,000 per customer

**Realistic LTV Estimate:** R 120,000-180,000 per customer

**Optimistic LTV Estimate:** R 180,000-360,000 per customer (enterprise customers)
