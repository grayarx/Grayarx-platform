# GrayArx Training Video Script

**Duration:** ~12-15 minutes  
**Tone:** Confident, knowledgeable, conversational (not robotic)  
**Audience:** Dealership owners/managers seeing GrayArx for the first time

---

**[You on camera, friendly but professional]**

We've built this specifically for South African dealerships. It's designed to work on your website and WhatsApp, in multiple languages, and it requires almost zero setup from you.

Let's dive in."

---

## SECTION 2: WHAT IS GRAYARX? (1.5 minutes)

**[Show homepage on screen]**

"GrayArx is an AI operating system for dealerships. Think of it as having a sales assistant that never sleeps. It's available 24/7 on your website and WhatsApp, responds to customers in their language, captures their information, and scores them by how likely they are to buy.

Here's what happens: A customer visits your website or messages you on WhatsApp. They ask about a car. Our AI responds immediately with information about that vehicle. If they're interested, we capture their details. You get notified. And you follow up.

All of this happens automatically. You don't need to do anything except onboard once and upload your inventory."

## SECTION 3: THE ONBOARDING PROCESS (2 minutes)

**[Show admin dashboard login]**

[Click through to settings]

You'll see your dealership information here. You add your name, your location, your business hours, and any special information you want customers to know. This takes about 5 minutes.

Next, you upload your inventory as a CSV file. This is just a spreadsheet with your cars—model, price, year, condition. You can export this from your inventory system or create it manually. We support any format."

**[Show CSV upload interface]**

"You click 'Upload CSV' and select your file. That's it. Our AI reads it and learns every car you have, every price, every detail."

## SECTION 4: HOW THE AI LEARNS YOUR INVENTORY (1.5 minutes)

**[Show dashboard with inventory section]**

"Here's where the magic happens. Once you upload your CSV, the AI doesn't just store it—it learns it. It understands your inventory at a deep level.

When a customer asks 'Do you have a Toyota Fortuner?', the AI checks your inventory and responds with the exact details: price, year, condition, everything.

If you have multiple Fortuners, it might say something like: 'Yes, we have two Toyota Fortuners in stock. One is a 2023 model for R450,000 and another is a 2022 model for R420,000. Which one interests you?'

The AI is trained on YOUR dealership's data. It knows your cars better than anyone."

## SECTION 5: LEAD CAPTURE IN ACTION (2 minutes)

**[Show chatbot interface on website]**

"Now let's see what customers experience. They visit your website and see the GrayArx chatbot in the corner. They click it and start a conversation.

[Show example conversation]

Customer: 'Hi, do you have any SUVs?'

AI: 'Yes, we have several SUVs available. We have Toyota Fortuners, Hyundai Santa Fes, and Ford Rangers. What's your budget?'

Customer: 'I'm looking for something around R400,000.'

AI: 'Great. Based on your budget, I'd recommend our 2023 Toyota Fortuner at R420,000 or our 2022 Hyundai Santa Fe at R380,000. Would you like to know more about either of these?'

Customer: 'Tell me about the Fortuner.'

AI: 'The 2023 Toyota Fortuner is in excellent condition. It has 15,000 km on the clock, full service history, and comes with a 3-year warranty. It's priced at R420,000. Would you like to schedule a test drive?'

Customer: 'Yes, I'd like to schedule a test drive.'

[Lead capture form appears]

AI: 'Perfect. I'll need your name, email, phone number, and preferred time. What works best for you?'

Customer enters their information and boom—you get notified immediately. The lead is in your dashboard."

## SECTION 7: MANAGING YOUR INVENTORY - THE SOLD CAR FEATURE (1.5 minutes)

**[Show inventory management section]**

"Here's something important. Your inventory changes. You sell cars. You get new cars. You need to keep GrayArx updated so the AI doesn't try to sell cars you don't have anymore.

[Show inventory list]

Let's say you sold that Toyota Fortuner. You come here, find it in your inventory list, and click 'Mark as Sold'.

[Click the button]

That's it. The AI immediately knows that car is no longer available. If a customer asks about it, the AI will say 'That vehicle has been sold, but we have similar options available.'

The sold car disappears from the AI's recommendations. It won't try to sell something you don't have. And if you get a new Fortuner in stock, you just upload an updated CSV and the AI learns about it immediately."

## RECORDING NOTES FOR YOU

**When you record this script:**

1. **Be conversational, not robotic.** This isn't a corporate video. It's you explaining your product. Sound like you know what you're talking about (because you do).

2. **Speak naturally.** Don't memorize word-for-word. Use the script as a guide and speak in your own words. Pauses are fine. Natural is better than perfect.

3. **Show the actual system.** Record your screen as you go through the dashboard. Show real examples. Make it tangible.

4. **Use your test CSV.** When you show inventory upload, use a real CSV with your test cars. When you show lead capture, show a real example conversation.

5. **Don't worry about "um" and "uh".** I'll clean those up. Just focus on explaining clearly.

6. **Smile when you talk.** Even though it's screen recording, people can hear it in your voice.

7. **Speak clearly and at a normal pace.** Not too fast, not too slow. Imagine you're explaining this to a friend.

8. **Sections you can skip or shorten:**
   - If WhatsApp isn't live yet, skip Section 9 or say "Coming soon"
   - If analytics aren't fully built, shorten Section 10
   - Adjust based on what's actually ready
