Section 01

Executive Summary

GrayArx is South Africa's first AI-powered Dealership Operating System — a single platform that replaces the fragmented stack of CRMs, call centres, and manual follow-up processes that cost dealerships millions in lost leads every year. Seven specialised AI agents handle sales, prospecting, email, WhatsApp, invoicing, auditing, and follow-up — around the clock, 365 days a year.

The Problem

South African car dealerships lose an estimated 60–70% of inbound leads that arrive outside business hours. A prospect who messages a dealership at 9 PM on a Sunday receives silence — and buys from a competitor by Monday morning. This is not a people problem; it is a structural one. Dealerships simply cannot afford to staff 24/7 sales coverage.

The same dynamic plays out in every emerging market from Lagos to Jakarta: WhatsApp is the primary business channel, digital infrastructure lags behind consumer demand, and dealerships have no scalable way to capture and nurture leads at the speed customers expect.

The Solution

GrayArx deploys a fleet of AI agents that respond instantly, qualify leads, book test drives, send pre-approval links, and follow up persistently — all through WhatsApp, webchat, and email simultaneously. A dealership using GrayArx never loses a lead to after-hours silence again.

Current Traction

GrayArx is currently in active pilot phase, onboarding its first cohort of South African dealerships via a zero-commitment 7-day free trial. The platform is live with WhatsApp integration (Meta Cloud API), webchat, email agents, vehicle inventory management, lead tracking, and booking functionality. All data handling is POPIA-compliant.

The Vision

South Africa is the proving ground. The real opportunity is the 50,000+ car dealerships across Sub-Saharan Africa, MENA, Southeast Asia, and Latin America — all operating in WhatsApp-first economies, all underserved by legacy US-built CRM tools, and all facing the same after-hours lead crisis. GrayArx will become the global dealership OS for emerging markets, the same way Shopify became the commerce OS for SMEs worldwide.

Section 02

Market Opportunity

South Africa — Home Market

South Africa's automotive retail industry is one of the largest on the African continent, generating over R400 billion in annual turnover. There are more than 11,000 registered dealerships across the country, ranging from large franchise groups (Motus, WeBuyCars, Unitrans) to independent township dealers.

GrayArx's initial target segment is the 500 highest-density dealerships in Gauteng, the Western Cape, and KwaZulu-Natal — the three provinces that account for roughly 70% of new and used vehicle sales. These dealerships share a common pain: digital-savvy consumers who message via WhatsApp and expect instant responses, met by dealerships still relying on salespeople and voicemail.

TAM (South Africa): 11,000 dealerships × avg R5,000/month = R660M ARR potential in South Africa alone.

Phase 2 — Sub-Saharan Africa

Beyond South Africa, Sub-Saharan Africa presents a compellingly underserved market. Nigeria alone has an estimated 4,000+ used-car dealers; Kenya's car market is growing at double digits annually; Ghana, Tanzania, and Zambia are emerging hubs. These markets share three key characteristics that make them ideal for GrayArx:

  • WhatsApp-first: WhatsApp is used for business transactions, customer service, and sales by default
  • Minimal existing infrastructure: No dominant CRM or DMS has captured these markets
  • Mobile-first consumers: Buyers expect instant mobile responses; gaps in service are commercially costly

Phase 3 — MENA, Southeast Asia & Latin America

The MENA automotive market exceeded $200 billion in 2025. Southeast Asia — Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand — represents a further 500,000+ car transactions annually with WhatsApp as the dominant communication layer. Brazil and Mexico together account for over 4 million new vehicles sold per year, with WhatsApp the primary sales channel for used cars.

Go-to-Market Strategy

The Pilot Flywheel

GrayArx's primary acquisition engine is the 7-day free pilot. A dealership onboards with zero friction, zero credit card, and zero technical setup. Within 48 hours they see AI agents responding to real leads. The emotional shift — watching a bot close a booking at 2 AM — is the most powerful sales tool in the arsenal.

Day 0

Onboarding

Dealer signs up, connects WhatsApp number, imports vehicle inventory via CSV. Setup takes under 30 minutes.

Day 1–3

Live agents activated

Nala (webchat), Bongi (WhatsApp), and Lerato (email) go live. Sipho begins outbound prospecting on the dealer's behalf. All interactions logged to the dashboard.

Day 4–6

Proof accumulates

Dealer receives weekly report: leads captured after hours, bookings made, messages handled. Kagiso (audit agent) surfaces insights and ROI estimates.

Day 7

Conversion conversation

Sales team contacts the dealer with personalised data. Pilot dealerships that saw at least one after-hours booking convert at high rates — the evidence does the selling.

South African Market

There is currently no direct competitor in South Africa combining AI agents, WhatsApp integration, and inventory management in a single platform at an accessible price point. The closest alternatives are:

  • AutoTrader SA / Cars.co.za: Listing portals, not a dealership OS. They generate leads but do not manage them.
  • Salesforce / HubSpot: Generic CRMs not configured for automotive. Expensive. No WhatsApp automation. Requires substantial implementation cost.
  • Local DMS providers (DMS Africa, DealerFloor): Back-office systems focused on stock and accounting, not AI sales agents.

First-mover advantage is real and time-limited. GrayArx has an 18–24 month window to establish network effects and customer lock-in before better-funded competitors discover this gap. Speed of execution is the moat.

Defensible Moats

Data Network Effects

Every conversation, every lead, every booking trains GrayArx's models on African automotive buyer behaviour — data no US competitor can replicate.

WhatsApp-Native Architecture

Built from day one on Meta Cloud API. Retrofitting WhatsApp into legacy CRMs is painful; replicating a WhatsApp-first platform is a 12+ month engineering project.

Multi-Agent Coordination

Seven AI agents that hand off leads between each other (Bongi → Lerato → Mia → Thandi) create a workflow that single-agent systems cannot match.

Local Relationships

SA automotive is a relationship-driven industry. GrayArx's team understands the culture, speaks the language, and knows the gatekeepers at major dealer groups.
