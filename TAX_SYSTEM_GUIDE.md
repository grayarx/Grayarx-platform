# GrayArx Tax System Guide

## Overview

The GrayArx Tax Dashboard is a **founder-only** administrative tool that helps you manage your South African tax obligations and maximize deductions. This system is **not accessible to dealerships** and is designed specifically for solo founder operations.

---

## Key Concepts

### 1. Drawings vs. Salary

**Drawings** (Recommended for you):
- Money you take from company profits
- No PAYE/UIF withholding
- Simple process: track income → deduct expenses → calculate tax → take the rest
- Best for solo founders with no employees
- Requires: proper documentation and annual ITR12 filing

**Salary** (For future employees):
- Regular monthly payment to yourself or employees
- Subject to PAYE withholding (18-45% depending on amount)
- Subject to UIF (1% capped at R177.12/month)
- Requires: monthly PAYE payments to SARS, quarterly EMP201 filing
- Use this when you hire employees

### 2. Tax Calculation (Solo Founder)

```
Monthly Income (from dealership campaigns)
- Business Expenses (deductible)
= Taxable Income
× 28% (corporate tax rate)
= Tax Owed
= After-Tax Profit (available to draw)
```

### 3. Deductible Business Expenses

All legitimate business expenses reduce your taxable income. Common deductions:

**Technology & Equipment:**
- Computer, laptop, monitor
- Software licenses (Manus, accounting software, etc.)
- Internet & phone (business portion)
- Cloud storage & backup

**Home Office (Section 23(b)):**
- Rent/lease (business portion only)
- Electricity (business portion)
- Water (business portion)
- Internet (100% if business use)
- Insurance (business portion)
- Maintenance & repairs (business portion)
- Property tax/rates (business portion)

**Professional Services:**
- Accounting & bookkeeping
- Legal fees (business-related)
- Tax consulting
- Audit fees

**Other Deductions:**
- Office supplies & stationery
- Professional development & training
- Advertising & marketing
- Business insurance
- Bank fees & charges

### 4. Home Office Deduction

The home office deduction is calculated using **floor area apportionment**:

```
Deductible Amount = Monthly Expense × (Office Area m² / Total Home Area m²)
```

**Example:**
- Office: 20 m²
- Home: 100 m²
- Apportionment: 20% (20 ÷ 100)

If your monthly rent is R10,000:
- Deductible: R10,000 × 20% = R2,000/month

**Requirements:**
- Office must be **regularly and exclusively** used for business
- Must be **specifically equipped** for business purposes
- Duties must be **mainly** performed there (>50%)
- Keep photos and floor plan as proof

**Important:** Mortgage interest is **NO LONGER deductible** (changed March 2022). Only rent, utilities, insurance, and maintenance are deductible.

### 5. Tax Savings Example

**Without Deductions:**
- Monthly Income: R100,000
- Tax: R100,000 × 28% = R28,000
- After-tax profit: R72,000

**With Deductions:**
- Monthly Income: R100,000
- Home Office: R2,000
- Computer/Software: R1,500
- Internet: R800
- Professional Services: R1,000
- **Total Deductions: R5,300**
- Taxable Income: R94,700
- Tax: R94,700 × 28% = R26,516
- **Tax Savings: R1,484/month** (20% reduction!)
- After-tax profit: R73,484

## Using the Tax Dashboard

### 1. Monthly Reconciliation

Every month:
1. Enter your total income (from dealership campaigns)
2. Enter all business expenses with receipts
3. Calculate home office deduction
4. System calculates tax owed and available drawing amount
5. Take the after-tax profit as a drawing

### 2. Tracking Expenses

Keep records of:
- All business receipts (invoices, receipts, bank statements)
- Monthly utility bills (electricity, water, internet)
- Professional service invoices
- Software subscription receipts
- Any other business-related expenses

### 3. Home Office Setup

To claim home office deduction:
1. Measure your office area (m²)
2. Measure your total home area (m²)
3. Calculate percentage (office ÷ total)
4. Enter monthly costs (rent, electricity, water, internet, insurance, maintenance)
5. System calculates deductible amount

### 4. Annual ITR12 Filing

The system prepares your annual tax return:
- Total annual income
- Total annual deductions
- Taxable income
- Estimated tax
- Due date: 29 May following year

## SARS Compliance

### Key Deadlines

**Annual:**
- **ITR12 Filing:** Due 29 May following year
- **Provisional Tax:** Due 28 Feb and 31 Aug (if income > R1M)

**Monthly:**
- Keep all receipts and bank statements
- Maintain business expense records
- Track home office usage

### Documentation Required

Keep for at least 5 years:
- Bank statements (business & personal)
- All invoices & receipts
- Home office photos & floor plan
- Vehicle logbook (if claiming vehicle deduction)
- Payroll records (if you hire employees)
- Email confirmations & contracts

### Audit Protection

To protect yourself in a SARS audit:
1. Keep all original receipts (not just bank statements)
2. Maintain detailed expense records with dates
3. Document home office setup (photos, measurements)
4. Keep business purpose notes for questionable expenses
5. Use the Tax Dashboard to generate audit-ready reports

## Future Scenarios

### When You Hire Your First Employee

Once you hire an employee:
1. Register for PAYE
2. Register for UIF
3. Monthly PAYE calculations change (see tax tables)
4. File EMP201 quarterly reconciliation
5. Pay PAYE by 7th of each month
6. Skills Development Levy kicks in (if payroll > R500k/year)

The Tax Dashboard will support this transition.

### When You Get VAT Registered

When your income exceeds R1M/year:
1. You can register for VAT (15%)
2. You charge VAT to customers
3. You recover VAT on business expenses
4. Vehicle deductions become more valuable (recover VAT on purchase)
5. Monthly VAT returns required

The Tax Dashboard will support VAT calculations.

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I deduct my home rent?**
A: Yes, but only the business portion. Calculate: (Office m² ÷ Total Home m²) × Monthly Rent

**Q: Can I deduct my mortgage interest?**
A: No, mortgage interest is no longer deductible (changed March 2022). Only rent is deductible.

**Q: Can I deduct my car?**
A: Only the business portion. You must keep a logbook tracking business km. Deductible: (Business km ÷ Total km) × Total Expenses

**Q: What if I don't have receipts?**
A: SARS requires original receipts. Bank statements alone are not sufficient proof. Always keep receipts.

**Q: How much tax should I set aside?**
A: 28% of your taxable income (after deductions). The Tax Dashboard calculates this automatically.

**Q: Can I take more than the after-tax profit as a drawing?**
A: No, that would mean taking money that belongs to SARS. Only take the after-tax profit.

**Q: What happens if I don't file ITR12?**
A: SARS can impose penalties (up to 200% of tax owed) and interest. Always file by 29 May.
