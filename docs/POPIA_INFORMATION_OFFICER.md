# POPIA Information Officer — what it means for GrayArx

Under the **Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA)**, every organisation that processes personal information must appoint an **Information Officer (IO)**.

## For GrayArx (Pty) Ltd

| Item | GrayArx detail |
|------|----------------|
| **Who is the IO?** | Typically the CEO / founder (Henrique) unless you formally delegate |
| **Public contact** | `privacy@grayarx.com` (listed in Privacy Policy) |
| **Role** | Ensures POPIA compliance, handles data subject requests, liaises with the Information Regulator |

## Registration (your action — not automatic)

1. Go to the **Information Regulator of South Africa**: [inforegulator.org.za](https://www.justice.gov.za/inforeg/regulator.html)
2. Register GrayArx as a **responsible party** and name the Information Officer
3. Keep proof of registration on file (PDF/email confirmation)
4. Ensure `privacy@grayarx.com` is monitored — use `/admin/compliance` + Gmail forwarding

**This is not “illegal” without registration** for a tiny pilot, but it **is required** before you scale or if the Regulator enquires. Budget half a day with your attorney if unsure.

## Dealership vs GrayArx

- **Each dealership** is the **responsible party** for its customers’ data
- **GrayArx** is the **operator/processor** on the dealer’s instructions
- Dealers sign the **POPIA Consent Form** at `/legal/popia-consent-form` acknowledging this split

## What GrayArx already does in product

- Privacy policy with IO contact
- POPIA consent at signup and onboarding
- Compliance contact form + monitored inbox
- Data subject request tooling under `compliance.popia` (dealer console)
- 30-day response commitment in Privacy Policy

## Annual checklist

- [ ] IO registration current with Information Regulator
- [ ] `privacy@grayarx.com` monitored daily
- [ ] Signed POPIA forms on file per pilot dealer
- [ ] Breach notification process documented (notify Regulator + subjects if required)
