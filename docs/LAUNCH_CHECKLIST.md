## Troubleshooting

**Issue:** PayFast approval delayed
- **Solution:** Use manual bank transfer in the meantime

**Issue:** Low email open rates
- **Solution:** A/B test subject lines, improve email copy

**Issue:** Dealerships can't upload inventory
- **Solution:** Check CSV format, provide template

**Issue:** Leads not being captured
- **Solution:** Verify honeypot is working, check rate limits
