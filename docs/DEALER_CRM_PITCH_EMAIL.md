## Studio photo background (product note for demos)

**What we built:** Every vehicle photo is automatically displayed on a consistent **dark studio backdrop** (spotlight on the floor, gold accent, vignette) — so mixed uploads (DMS exports, phone shots, imagin.studio renders) look uniform on your showroom and inventory.

**Where it works today:**
- Dealer inventory cards (`/dealer/inventory`)
- Public showroom (`/showroom`)
- Vehicle detail gallery
- Photo uploader previews (8-angle upload)

**How it works:** Upload any car image as usual — no extra editing. The system composites it on the studio frame at display time (`object-contain`, floor-aligned, drop shadow). No Photoshop step for the dealer.

**Demo line:** *"Drop your stock photos in — GrayArx makes them look like a luxury showroom listing automatically."*
