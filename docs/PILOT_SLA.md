# GrayArx Pilot SLA (honest summary)

**Audience:** Dealer calls / onboarding  
**Full legal:** [SERVICE_LEVEL_AGREEMENT.md](./legal/SERVICE_LEVEL_AGREEMENT.md)  
**Updated:** July 2026

Pilot support is founder-led. These are targets we can keep as a solo operator — not enterprise NOC theatre.

## Response targets (pilot)

| Priority | What it means | First response | Target fix / next step |
|----------|---------------|----------------|------------------------|
| **Critical** | Showroom / WhatsApp / login down for a live dealer | Within **4 business hours** (same day if reported before 14:00 SAST) | Workaround or restore same day when possible |
| **High** | Core feature broken (CSV, bookings, leads inbox) | Within **1 business day** | Fix or clear plan within **2–3 business days** |
| **Medium** | Degraded UX, non-blocking bugs | Within **2 business days** | Queued in Kagiso roadmap; founder-approved patches |
| **Low** | How-to, cosmetic, feature asks | Within **3 business days** | Best-effort / backlog |

Business hours: **Mon–Fri 08:00–18:00 SAST**. Critical WhatsApp/webhook issues: founder cell on the onboarding pack + **hello@grayarx.com**.

## What we commit to in pilot

- Acknowledge every dealer bug report (Help chat ticket or email).
- Kagiso opens an investigation + proposed fix; **founder approves** before production writes.
- Uptime best-effort on hosted app + DB; third-party outages (Meta, Stripe, OpenAI, email) are excluded.
- No fabricated 99.9% credit machinery during pilot — service credits remain discretionary.

## Contract options

| Option | Billing | Rate | Notes |
|--------|---------|------|-------|
| **Month-to-month** | Monthly | Negotiated pilot / then list | Cancel with **30 days’ notice** |
| **12-month commit** | Monthly or annual | **Founder rate lock** for the term | Prefer when Meta WA + multi-branch are in scope |

Exact ZAR and signatures live in the Dealer Agreement / POPIA pack — don’t invent numbers on the call.
