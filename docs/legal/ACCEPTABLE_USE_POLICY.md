# GrayArx Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective Date:** 1 June 2026  
**Last Updated:** 23 May 2026

---

## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("**Policy**") sets out the prohibited uses of the GrayArx platform and services. Violation of this Policy may result in account suspension or termination without refund.

---

## 2. Prohibited Activities

You agree NOT to use the GrayArx Service to:

### 2.1 Illegal Activities
- Violate any South African law or regulation
- Engage in fraud, forgery, or misrepresentation
- Facilitate money laundering, terrorism financing, or sanctions evasion
- Engage in bribery or corruption
- Violate intellectual property rights (copyright, trademark, patent)

### 2.2 Data Protection Violations
- Collect, store, or process personal information without lawful consent
- Violate POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act)
- Violate the NCA (National Credit Act) or credit regulations
- Violate the CPA (Consumer Protection Act)
- Violate the ECTA (Electronic Communications and Transactions Act)

### 2.3 Spam & Harassment
- Send unsolicited bulk communications (spam)
- Send communications to individuals who have opted out
- Impersonate another person or entity
- Harass, threaten, or defame any person
- Send communications containing hate speech or discrimination
- Engage in phishing, social engineering, or identity theft

### 2.4 Security & System Integrity
- Attempt to gain unauthorized access to the Service
- Reverse-engineer, decompile, or disassemble the Service
- Introduce malware, viruses, or other malicious code
- Conduct denial-of-service (DoS) attacks
- Circumvent security measures or rate limits
- Probe or scan for vulnerabilities
- Interfere with the Service's operation or infrastructure

### 2.5 Intellectual Property Violations
- Upload or distribute copyrighted content without authorization
- Upload or distribute trademarked content without authorization
- Violate open-source software licenses
- Claim ownership of content you do not own

### 2.6 Abusive Behavior
- Engage in harassment, bullying, or intimidation
- Make threats of violence or harm
- Engage in stalking or doxxing
- Engage in coordinated harassment campaigns
- Post content that sexualizes, exploits, or endangers minors

### 2.7 Fraud & Deception
- Engage in bait-and-switch tactics
- Misrepresent vehicle condition, price, or availability
- Engage in predatory lending or credit practices
- Engage in advance-fee fraud or other scams
- Provide false information in your account registration

### 2.8 Resale & Redistribution
- Resell or redistribute the Service without authorization
- Provide access to the Service to unauthorized third parties
- Use the Service as a platform for reselling other services
- Engage in arbitrage or price manipulation

### 2.9 Competitive Harm
- Use the Service to gather competitive intelligence
- Use the Service to monitor competitors' activities
- Use the Service to interfere with competitors' operations
- Engage in market manipulation or anti-competitive behavior

### 2.10 Abuse of AI Agents
- Use AI agents to generate misleading or deceptive communications
- Use AI agents to send spam or unsolicited communications
- Use AI agents to impersonate real persons or entities
- Use AI agents to generate content that violates this Policy
- Attempt to manipulate or "jailbreak" AI agents

## 3. Consequences of Violation

### 3.1 Warnings
For minor violations, we may issue a written warning and request corrective action within 14 days.

### 3.2 Suspension
For serious violations, we may suspend your account for up to 30 days. During suspension:
- You cannot access the Service
- Your data is retained
- Your subscription fees continue to accrue

### 3.3 Termination
For severe or repeated violations, we may terminate your account immediately. Upon termination:
- Your access to the Service is revoked
- Your data is deleted within 30 days
- You forfeit any remaining subscription fees
- You may be liable for damages

### 3.4 Legal Action
We may pursue legal action for violations that cause harm, including:
- Cease-and-desist letters
- Civil litigation for damages
- Referral to law enforcement for criminal prosecution
