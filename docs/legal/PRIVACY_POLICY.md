## 1. Introduction

GrayArx (Pty) Ltd ("**we**", "**us**", "**our**", or "**Company**") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our platform. ## 2. Definitions

- **"Personal Information"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person
- **"Processing"** means any operation performed on personal information (collection, storage, use, disclosure, deletion)
- **"Data Subject"** means the person to whom personal information relates
- **"Responsible Party"** means the entity that determines the purpose and means of processing personal information (GrayArx)
- **"Operator"** means the entity that processes personal information on behalf of the Responsible Party

## 7. Data Subject Rights (POPIA)

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you have the following rights:

### 7.1 Right of Access
You have the right to request access to your personal information. We will provide a copy within 15 business days.

### 7.2 Right to Correction
You have the right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.

### 7.3 Right to Deletion
You have the right to request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements.

### 7.4 Right to Object
You have the right to object to processing of your personal information for marketing purposes. We will honor opt-out requests within 48 hours.

### 7.5 Right to Data Portability
You have the right to request your personal information in a portable format (e.g., CSV, JSON) to transfer to another service.

### 7.6 Right to Lodge a Complaint
If you believe we have violated your privacy rights, you may lodge a complaint with the **Information Regulator** (South Africa's data protection authority).

**How to Exercise Your Rights:**
Email us at privacy@grayarx.com with:
- Your name and account email
- Description of your request
- Proof of identity (copy of ID or passport)

We will respond within 15 business days.

## 8. Data Security

### 8.1 Security Measures
We implement industry-standard security controls to protect your personal information:

- **Encryption:** Data in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256)
- **Access Control:** Role-based access, multi-factor authentication
- **Firewalls:** Network perimeter protection
- **Intrusion Detection:** Real-time threat monitoring
- **Backup & Recovery:** Daily automated backups, disaster recovery plan
- **Vulnerability Management:** Regular security audits and penetration testing
- **Employee Training:** Data protection and confidentiality training

### 8.2 Limitations
**No security system is impenetrable.** We cannot guarantee absolute security. You use the Service at your own risk. We are not liable for unauthorized access, data breaches, or loss of data due to circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

### 8.3 Data Breach Notification
In the event of a data breach, we will:
- Investigate the breach within 24 hours
- Notify affected data subjects within 3 business days (if required by POPIA)
- Notify the Information Regulator within 30 days
- Provide guidance on protective measures

## 9. Data Retention

### 9.1 Retention Periods

| Data Type | Retention Period | Reason |
|-----------|---|---|
| Account information | Duration of subscription + 30 days | Service delivery, legal compliance |
| Customer leads | Duration of subscription + 12 months | Dealership reference, dispute resolution |
| Communications (email, WhatsApp) | Duration of subscription + 6 months | Audit trail, customer service |
| Payment records | 7 years | Tax and financial reporting (SARS) |
| Server logs | 90 days | Security and performance monitoring |
| Cookies | 12 months (session) or 2 years (persistent) | User preference and analytics |
| Trade-in valuations | Duration of subscription + 12 months | Dealership reference |

### 9.2 Deletion Process
Upon account termination or deletion request:
1. Data is marked for deletion in our primary database
2. Data is removed from backups within 30 days
3. Data is securely wiped (not recoverable)

## 11. Cookies & Tracking

### 11.1 Types of Cookies

| Cookie Type | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Session | Authentication, user session | Session (browser close) |
| Preference | Language, theme, settings | 2 years |
| Analytics | Usage tracking, behavior analysis | 2 years |
| Marketing | Retargeting ads, conversion tracking | 1 year |

### 11.2 Cookie Control
You can control cookies through your browser settings:
- **Chrome:** Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
- **Firefox:** Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
- **Safari:** Preferences → Privacy → Cookies

**Note:** Disabling cookies may affect Service functionality.

### 11.3 Do Not Track
Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" signal. We do not currently respond to DNT signals, but you may opt-out of analytics tracking by emailing privacy@grayarx.com.
