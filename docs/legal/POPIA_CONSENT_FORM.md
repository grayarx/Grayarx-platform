## 3. POPIA Compliance Acknowledgments

### 3.1 Responsible Party Status
The dealership acknowledges that it is the **"Responsible Party"** under POPIA, meaning the dealership:
- **Determines the purpose and means** of processing personal information
- **Bears full responsibility** for POPIA compliance
- **Is liable** for any POPIA violations

GrayArx acts as a **"Processor"** on behalf of the dealership and processes personal information only as instructed.

### 3.2 Lawful Basis for Processing
The dealership confirms that all personal information processed through the GrayArx platform is collected and processed on one or more of the following lawful bases:

**☐ Consent** — The dealership has obtained explicit, informed consent from the data subject before collecting their personal information.

**☐ Contract** — The personal information is necessary to perform a contract with the data subject (e.g., vehicle purchase agreement, financing agreement).

**☐ Legal Obligation** — The dealership is required by law to collect and process the personal information (e.g., tax reporting, credit reporting).

**☐ Legitimate Interest** — The dealership has a legitimate business interest in processing the personal information that is not overridden by the data subject's rights (e.g., fraud prevention, customer service).

**☐ Other** — [Specify other lawful basis if applicable]

### 3.3 Consent Requirement
The dealership acknowledges that:
- **Explicit consent is required** before collecting sensitive personal information (race, religion, health, sexual orientation, criminal record, etc.)
- **Consent must be freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous**
- **Consent cannot be a condition** of receiving a service (except where necessary to provide the service)
- **Consent must be documented** and retained for audit purposes

### 3.4 Purpose Limitation
The dealership confirms that personal information will be processed **only for the purposes disclosed to the data subject**, including:
- Lead capture and customer relationship management
- Vehicle inventory and sales
- Customer communication and follow-up
- Finance and credit assessment
- Service and warranty management
- Regulatory compliance and reporting

**Further processing for different purposes requires new consent.**

### 3.5 Information Quality
The dealership commits to ensuring that all personal information processed is:
- **Accurate and complete** — Information is correct and not misleading
- **Up-to-date** — Information is kept current and corrected when necessary
- **Relevant** — Information is adequate and not excessive for the stated purpose
- **Not excessive** — Only necessary information is collected

### 3.6 Openness & Transparency
The dealership commits to:
- **Providing privacy notices** to data subjects before or at the time of collection
- **Disclosing the purpose** of processing
- **Identifying the Responsible Party** (dealership) and Processor (GrayArx)
- **Explaining data subject rights** (access, correction, deletion, objection)
- **Providing contact information** for privacy inquiries

## 4. Data Subject Rights

### 4.1 Right of Access
The dealership acknowledges that data subjects have the right to:
- Request access to their personal information
- Receive a copy of their personal information
- Know how their information is being used

The dealership commits to responding to access requests within **15 business days**.

### 4.2 Right to Correction
The dealership acknowledges that data subjects have the right to:
- Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
- Have incorrect information corrected or deleted

The dealership commits to correcting information within **15 business days** of request.

### 4.3 Right to Deletion
The dealership acknowledges that data subjects have the right to:
- Request deletion of their personal information
- Have their information deleted (subject to legal retention requirements)

The dealership commits to deleting information within **15 business days** of request (unless legally required to retain it).

### 4.4 Right to Object
The dealership acknowledges that data subjects have the right to:
- Object to processing of their personal information
- Opt-out of marketing communications
- Request cessation of processing

The dealership commits to honoring opt-out requests within **48 hours**.

### 4.5 Right to Data Portability
The dealership acknowledges that data subjects have the right to:
- Request their personal information in a portable format (CSV, JSON, etc.)
- Transfer their information to another service provider

The dealership commits to providing portable data within **15 business days** of request.

### 4.6 Right to Lodge a Complaint
The dealership acknowledges that data subjects have the right to:
- Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator
- Seek redress for POPIA violations

## 5. Data Security & Protection

### 5.1 Security Measures
The dealership acknowledges that GrayArx implements appropriate security measures, including:
- **Encryption** — Data in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256)
- **Access Control** — Role-based access, multi-factor authentication
- **Firewalls** — Network perimeter protection
- **Monitoring** — Real-time threat detection
- **Backup & Recovery** — Daily automated backups

### 5.2 Dealership Security Responsibilities
The dealership commits to:
- **Protecting login credentials** — Not sharing passwords or access tokens
- **Reporting breaches** — Notifying GrayArx immediately of any suspected breach
- **Monitoring access** — Ensuring only authorized personnel access the Service
- **Secure disposal** — Securely deleting data when no longer needed

### 5.3 Data Breach Notification
The dealership acknowledges that:
- **GrayArx will notify the dealership** of any data breach within 24 hours of discovery
- **The dealership is responsible** for notifying affected data subjects within 3 business days (if required by POPIA)
- **The dealership is responsible** for notifying the Information Regulator within 30 days (if required)

## Appendix A: POPIA Principles Summary

| Principle | Description |
|---|---|
| **Lawfulness** | Personal information must be processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently |
| **Purpose Limitation** | Information must be collected for a specific, explicit purpose and not further processed in a manner incompatible with that purpose |
| **Further Processing** | Further processing for a different purpose requires new consent |
| **Information Quality** | Information must be accurate, complete, up-to-date, and not excessive |
| **Openness** | Data subjects must be informed about processing activities |
| **Security** | Appropriate security measures must be implemented to protect personal information |
| **Data Subject Participation** | Data subjects have rights to access, correct, delete, and object to processing |
| **Accountability** | The Responsible Party must demonstrate compliance with POPIA |
