## 2. Trade-In Valuation Disclaimer

### 2.1 Illustrative Nature
Trade-in valuations provided by the GrayArx platform (via the Tumi agent) are **illustrative estimates only** and do not constitute:
- A binding offer to purchase
- A credit assessment
- A valuation for credit purposes
- A guarantee of any kind

### 2.2 Basis of Estimates
Trade-in estimates are based on:
- Historical market data (South African automotive market)
- Vehicle specifications provided by the user
- General condition assessments (not physical inspections)
- Automated algorithms and machine learning models

### 2.3 Limitations
Trade-in estimates may not reflect:
- Current market conditions or price fluctuations
- Regional market variations
- Specific vehicle condition (mechanical, cosmetic, accident history)
- Outstanding finance or liens on the vehicle
- Mileage accuracy or service history verification
- Supply and demand dynamics

### 2.4 Final Valuation
The final trade-in value is determined solely by the dealership and must be:
- Agreed upon in writing with the customer
- Based on a physical inspection of the vehicle
- Compliant with the National Credit Act (NCA)
- Fair and not discriminatory

### 2.5 No Credit Offer
Trade-in valuations do NOT constitute:
- An offer to provide credit
- A credit agreement
- A pre-approval for financing
- A commitment to purchase the vehicle

## 3. Finance Calculator Disclaimer

### 3.1 Illustrative Nature
The GrayArx Finance Calculator is a tool for **illustrative affordability assessment only** and does not constitute:
- A credit offer
- A credit agreement
- A pre-approval for financing
- A binding commitment of any kind

### 3.2 Calculation Basis
Finance calculations are based on:
- Vehicle price entered by the user
- Deposit amount entered by the user
- Loan term (months) selected by the user
- Interest rate entered by the user
- Standard reducing-balance amortization formula

### 3.3 Limitations
Finance calculations may not reflect:
- Actual interest rates offered by lenders
- Actual lending criteria or affordability assessments
- Actual monthly payments (which may include insurance, warranties, etc.)
- Actual credit approval likelihood
- Changes in interest rates or market conditions
- Individual creditworthiness or credit history

### 3.4 Affordability Assessment
The Finance Calculator provides a color-coded affordability indicator based on:
- Monthly payment as a percentage of gross income
- Standard affordability thresholds (typically 25–35% of gross income)
- General lending guidelines (not specific lender criteria)

**The affordability indicator is illustrative only and does not guarantee credit approval.**

### 3.5 No Credit Offer
The Finance Calculator does NOT constitute:
- An offer to provide credit
- A credit agreement
- A pre-approval for financing
- A commitment to lend
- A guarantee of credit approval

### 4.2 NCA Compliance
GrayArx is NOT:
- A credit provider or credit intermediary
- Responsible for NCA compliance
- Liable for credit-related disputes or complaints
- Responsible for assessing customer affordability

### 4.3 Credit Agreements
Any credit agreement must be:
- Concluded separately from the GrayArx platform
- In writing and signed by both parties
- Compliant with the NCA
