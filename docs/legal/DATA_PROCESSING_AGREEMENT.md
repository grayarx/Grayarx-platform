# GrayArx Data Processing Agreement

**Effective Date:** 1 June 2026  
**Last Updated:** 23 May 2026

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope

This Data Processing Agreement ("**DPA**") sets out the terms and conditions under which GrayArx (Pty) Ltd ("**Processor**") processes personal information on behalf of the Customer ("**Responsible Party**") in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

This DPA applies to all personal information processed through the GrayArx Service, including:
- Customer contact information
- Vehicle inventory data
- Communications (emails, WhatsApp, voice)
- Transaction records
- Usage analytics

---

## 2. Definitions

- **"Personal Information"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person
- **"Processing"** means any operation performed on personal information (collection, storage, use, disclosure, deletion)
- **"Data Subject"** means the person to whom personal information relates
- **"Responsible Party"** means the Customer, who determines the purpose and means of processing
- **"Processor"** means GrayArx, who processes personal information on behalf of the Responsible Party
- **"Sub-processor"** means a third party engaged by the Processor to process personal information (e.g., cloud hosts, email providers)

---

## 3. Roles & Responsibilities

### 3.1 Responsible Party (Customer) Responsibilities
The Customer is responsible for:
- Determining the purpose and means of processing
- Obtaining explicit consent from data subjects before processing
- Providing privacy notices to data subjects
- Ensuring processing complies with POPIA and other applicable laws
- Responding to data subject requests (access, correction, deletion)
- Notifying the Processor of data breaches
- Maintaining records of processing activities

### 3.2 Processor (GrayArx) Responsibilities
GrayArx is responsible for:
- Processing personal information only as instructed by the Customer
- Implementing appropriate security measures
- Ensuring sub-processors comply with this DPA
- Assisting the Customer in responding to data subject requests
- Notifying the Customer of data breaches
- Deleting or returning personal information upon termination
- Maintaining records of processing activities

---

## 4. Processing Instructions

### 4.1 Scope of Processing
GrayArx processes personal information for the following purposes:
- **Service Delivery:** Creating accounts, managing inventory, processing leads, sending communications
- **AI Agent Operations:** Training and improving AI agents, analyzing communication patterns
- **Analytics:** Measuring usage, identifying trends, improving the Service
- **Legal Compliance:** Responding to law enforcement, tax reporting, dispute resolution

### 4.2 Processing Limitations
GrayArx will NOT:
- Process personal information for purposes other than those listed above without prior written consent
- Transfer personal information outside South Africa without the Customer's consent
- Combine personal information from different data subjects without authorization
- Use personal information for profiling or automated decision-making (except for spam detection)

### 4.3 Data Subject Rights
GrayArx will assist the Customer in fulfilling data subject requests:
- **Access requests:** Provide a copy of personal information within 10 business days
- **Correction requests:** Correct inaccurate or incomplete information within 10 business days
- **Deletion requests:** Delete personal information within 10 business days (subject to legal retention requirements)
- **Objection requests:** Honor opt-out requests within 48 hours

## 5. Security Measures

### 5.1 Technical Safeguards
GrayArx implements the following technical security measures:
- **Encryption:** Data in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256)
- **Access Control:** Role-based access, multi-factor authentication, password policies
- **Firewalls:** Network perimeter protection, intrusion detection
- **Monitoring:** Real-time threat detection, security alerts
- **Backup & Recovery:** Daily automated backups, disaster recovery plan

### 5.2 Organizational Safeguards
GrayArx implements the following organizational security measures:
- **Personnel Training:** Annual data protection and confidentiality training
- **Access Restrictions:** Limited access to personal information (need-to-know basis)
- **Confidentiality Agreements:** All employees and contractors sign confidentiality agreements
- **Incident Response:** Documented procedures for responding to security incidents
- **Audit & Compliance:** Regular security audits, penetration testing, compliance reviews

### 5.3 Physical Safeguards
GrayArx implements the following physical security measures:
- **Data Centers:** Secure facilities with access controls, surveillance, fire suppression
- **Equipment:** Secure disposal of hardware containing personal information
- **Offices:** Restricted access, visitor logs, secure document storage

### 7.1 International Transfers
Personal information may be transferred to sub-processors located outside South Africa. GrayArx ensures:
- Adequate legal protections exist in the destination country
- Standard contractual clauses or other approved mechanisms are in place
- The Customer's explicit consent is obtained (if required)

### 7.2 Restricted Transfers
Personal information will NOT be transferred to countries that do not provide adequate data protection without the Customer's prior written consent.

## 8. Data Breach Notification

### 8.1 Notification Obligations
If a data breach occurs, GrayArx will:
1. Investigate the breach immediately
2. Notify the Customer within 24 hours of discovery
3. Provide details of:
   - Nature and scope of the breach
   - Personal information affected
   - Likely consequences
   - Measures taken to address the breach
   - Contact person for further information

### 8.2 Customer Notification
The Customer is responsible for notifying affected data subjects and the Information Regulator within the timeframes required by POPIA. GrayArx will assist as requested.

The Customer is responsible for responding to data subject requests. GrayArx will:
- Receive requests on behalf of the Customer (if directed)

### 9.2 Response Timelines
GrayArx will respond to requests within:

## 10. Data Retention & Deletion

### 10.1 Retention Periods
GrayArx retains personal information for the periods specified in the Privacy Policy and as required by law (e.g., tax records for 7 years).

### 10.2 Deletion Upon Termination
Upon termination of the Service, GrayArx will:
1. Provide the Customer with a copy of personal information (if requested)

GrayArx may retain personal information if:
- Required by law (tax, financial reporting, law enforcement)
- Necessary for legal proceedings or dispute resolution
- Requested by the Customer in writing

## 15. Compliance with POPIA

### 15.1 POPIA Requirements
GrayArx complies with the following POPIA requirements:
- **Lawfulness:** Processing is based on lawful grounds (consent, contract, legal obligation, legitimate interest)
- **Purpose Limitation:** Processing is limited to stated purposes
- **Further Processing:** Further processing requires the Customer's authorization
- **Information Quality:** Personal information is accurate, complete, and up-to-date
- **Openness:** The Customer and data subjects are informed of processing activities
- **Security:** Appropriate security measures are implemented
- **Data Subject Participation:** Data subjects can exercise their rights (access, correction, deletion)
- **Accountability:** GrayArx maintains records and can demonstrate compliance

### 15.2 POPIA Compliance Checklist
GrayArx maintains a POPIA compliance checklist covering:
- Lawful basis for processing
- Consent documentation
- Privacy notices
- Data subject requests
- Security measures
- Data breach incidents
- Sub-processor agreements
