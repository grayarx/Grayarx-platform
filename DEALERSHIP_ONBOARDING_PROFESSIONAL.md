# GrayArx Dealership Onboarding Program

## Program Overview

The GrayArx Dealership Onboarding Program is a structured 30-day implementation process designed to ensure successful platform deployment, rapid value realization, and sustainable operational integration. The program balances rapid implementation with comprehensive training and support.

---

## Implementation Timeline

### Week 1: Foundation & Configuration

**Day 1-2: Account Provisioning & Initial Setup**
- Account creation and access provisioning
- Dashboard familiarization and navigation training
- Security configuration (API keys, webhook setup)
- Initial system diagnostics and health checks

**Day 3-4: Data Integration**
- Vehicle inventory data import (CSV upload)
- Dealership profile configuration (hours, location, contact info)
- AI chatbot customization (tone, language preferences)
- Integration testing and validation

**Day 5-7: Testing & Validation**
- Sandbox testing environment activation
- End-to-end workflow testing (lead capture → notification → CRM)
- Performance baseline establishment
- Quality assurance sign-off

---

### Week 2: Deployment & Go-Live

**Day 8-10: Production Deployment**
- Production environment activation
- Live lead capture initiation
- Real-time monitoring and optimization
- Initial performance metrics collection

**Day 11-14: Operational Stabilization**
- Lead volume and quality monitoring
- Response time optimization
- Customer support ticket resolution
- Performance tuning and adjustments

---

### Week 3: Optimization & Scaling

**Day 15-21: Performance Analysis & Optimization**
- Lead quality assessment and scoring validation
- Conversion rate analysis
- Customer feedback collection
- System optimization recommendations

**Optimization Focus Areas:**
- Lead scoring accuracy (adjust thresholds if needed)
- Response time optimization
- Language accuracy and tone refinement
- Integration performance tuning

---

### Week 4: Sustainability & Transition

**Day 22-28: Handoff & Sustainability**
- Dealership team training on dashboard management
- Ongoing support transition to standard support channels
- Performance review and goal-setting for Month 2
- Escalation procedure documentation

**Day 29-30: Final Review & Commitment Decision**
- Comprehensive 30-day performance review
- ROI calculation and business impact assessment
- Tier selection and contract negotiation (if applicable)
- Ongoing support plan establishment

---

## Support Tiers During Onboarding

### Starter Tier Support

**Included:**
- Email support (24-48 hour response)
- Self-service knowledge base access
- Video tutorial library
- Community forum access
- Weekly automated performance reports

**Typical Response Time:** 24-48 hours
**Escalation Path:** Support team → Technical team

### Professional Tier Support

**Included (Starter features, plus):**
- Priority email support (12-24 hour response)
- Dedicated onboarding specialist
- Weekly check-in calls (first 4 weeks)
- Custom integration support
- Real-time performance monitoring

**Typical Response Time:** 12-24 hours
**Escalation Path:** Onboarding specialist → Technical team → Management

### Enterprise Tier Support

**Included (Professional features, plus):**
- Dedicated account manager
- Priority phone support (4-8 hour response)
- Bi-weekly business review calls
- Custom development support
- Direct access to engineering team

**Typical Response Time:** 4-8 hours
**Escalation Path:** Account manager → Engineering team → VP of Operations

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Lead Metrics

**Lead Volume**
- Target: 35-50 leads/month (Starter), 50-75 (Professional), 75-100+ (Enterprise)
- Measurement: Total qualified leads captured
- Frequency: Daily tracking, weekly reporting

**Lead Quality**
- Target: 25-35% lead-to-test-drive conversion
- Measurement: Leads that convert to dealership test drives
- Frequency: Weekly assessment

**Response Time**
- Target: <2 minutes average
- Measurement: Time from customer inquiry to AI response
- Frequency: Real-time monitoring

### System Metrics

**Platform Uptime**
- Target: 99.9% availability
- Measurement: System availability percentage
- Frequency: Real-time monitoring

**API Performance**
- Target: <500ms average response time
- Measurement: API endpoint response times
- Frequency: Real-time monitoring

**Webhook Delivery**
- Target: 99.5%+ delivery success rate
- Measurement: Successful webhook deliveries vs. failures
- Frequency: Real-time monitoring

### Business Metrics

**Customer Satisfaction**
- Target: 4.5+/5.0 rating
- Measurement: Post-implementation survey
- Frequency: Weekly during onboarding, monthly ongoing

**Support Quality**
- Target: <2% support ticket rate
- Measurement: Support tickets per 100 leads
- Frequency: Weekly tracking

**Churn Risk**
- Target: <5% monthly churn
- Measurement: Dealerships canceling subscriptions
- Frequency: Monthly assessment

## Onboarding Challenges & Solutions

### Challenge: Inaccurate Vehicle Data

**Solution:** CSV auto-repair engine automatically corrects common errors (typos, formatting, missing fields). Dealership reviews and confirms corrections before import.

### Challenge: Integration Complexity

**Solution:** Professional and Enterprise tiers include dedicated integration support. Pre-built connectors for common CRM systems (Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive).

### Challenge: Low Initial Lead Volume

**Solution:** Lead scoring thresholds adjusted, chatbot tone optimized, language accuracy improved. Most dealerships see 35-50 leads within first week.

### Challenge: Dealership Team Adoption

**Solution:** Comprehensive training program, video tutorials, knowledge base, and ongoing support. Weekly check-ins during first month.

### Challenge: Performance Expectations

**Solution:** Clear KPI setting during onboarding, weekly performance reviews, transparent reporting, and realistic goal-setting.
