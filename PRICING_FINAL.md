# GrayArx Pricing - Final Version

**Effective:** June 2026  
**Pricing Model:** Monthly Subscription (No Long-Term Contracts)  
**Billing:** Month-to-month, cancel anytime

---

## Pricing Tiers

### Tier 1: Starter — R3,999/month

**Perfect for:** Small dealerships, testing the platform, basic lead capture

**Includes:**
- AI Chatbot (24/7) on website & WhatsApp
- Unlimited lead capture (no limits)
- Multi-language support (English, Afrikaans, Zulu, Xhosa, and more)
- Lead scoring (AI rates lead quality automatically)
- Dashboard access (view all leads in one place)
- Email notifications (get alerted when new leads come in)
- CSV export (download leads anytime)
- Email support (24-48 hour response)
- Onboarding & setup (one-time configuration)
- Training videos & documentation
- Basic analytics (lead count, response times, channel breakdown)

**What's NOT included:**
- Phone support
- Dedicated account manager
- API access
- CRM integrations

**Monthly Cost to You:** R2,000  
**Your Profit:** R1,999 (50% margin)

---

### Tier 2: Professional — R7,999/month

**Perfect for:** Growing dealerships, those with CRM systems, advanced users

**Includes everything in Starter, plus:**
- AI learns your inventory automatically (upload CSV, AI knows every car)
- Advanced analytics & reporting (detailed insights into your leads)
- Lead prioritization (AI ranks leads by likelihood to buy)
- Webhook integration support (technical guidance to connect to your CRM)
- Guided setup call (30-minute Zoom to configure your account)
- Priority email support (12-24 hour response)
- Advanced training (video tutorials + live Q&A)
- Performance reports (trends over time)

**What's NOT included:**
- Phone support
- Real-time CRM sync
- Conversion tracking
- Dedicated account manager

**Monthly Cost to You:** R2,000  
**Your Profit:** R5,999 (75% margin)

---

### Tier 3: Enterprise — R11,999/month

**Perfect for:** Large dealerships, complex integrations, API-first approach

**Includes everything in Professional, plus:**
- API access (direct access to your data for developers)
- Inventory sync support (help connecting your inventory system)
- Custom webhook setup (hands-on technical support)
- Priority support (6-12 hour response time)
- Dedicated support channel (direct email/Slack for urgent issues)
- Quarterly performance reports (detailed analysis of your leads)
- Data upload management (we help organize and validate your data)
- Custom analytics (dashboards tailored to your needs)
- Bulk operations (upload/manage large amounts of data)

**What's NOT included:**
- Phone support
- Dedicated account manager
- Quarterly business reviews
- Custom feature development
- White-label solution

**Monthly Cost to You:** R2,500  
**Your Profit:** R9,499 (79% margin)

## Handling Common Objections

### "Why R3,999/month?"

**Response:** "You're not paying for a person—you're getting a 24/7 AI that captures leads in multiple languages. If you hired one person for this, it would cost R20,000+/month. Plus, our AI works while you sleep."

### "Can you integrate with my CRM?"

**Response:** "Yes! We support CSV export, webhooks, and API access. For Starter tier, you can export leads manually. Professional tier includes webhook guidance. Enterprise includes full API access."

### "What if I want to cancel?"

**Response:** "No long-term contracts. Cancel anytime. Your data is always available for export."

### "How do I know it will work for my dealership?"

**Response:** "We offer onboarding and training videos. Plus, you can start with Starter tier and upgrade if needed. We're confident you'll see results."

### "What about customer support?"

**Response:** "You get email support with response times based on your tier. Starter is 24-48 hours, Professional is 12-24 hours, Enterprise is 6-12 hours. We also provide onboarding and training."
