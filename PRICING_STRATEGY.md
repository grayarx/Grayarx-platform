## Executive Summary

GrayArx offers a flexible pricing model that serves dealerships at every stage of growth. The strategy balances accessibility for single-location startups with comprehensive solutions for large dealership groups. This document outlines both modular (à la carte) and bundled (tiered) pricing approaches, with recommendations for market entry and scaling.

**Recommended Strategy:** Launch with bundled tiers (Starter/Professional/Enterprise) as primary offering, with modular pricing available for custom requirements. This approach maximizes adoption while maintaining clear value communication.

## Market Context

### South African Dealership Market

The South African automotive dealership sector comprises approximately **3,000-5,000 active dealerships**, distributed across major metropolitan areas (Johannesburg, Cape Town, Durban) and secondary markets. The market segments into three primary categories:

**Tier 1 - Large Dealership Groups:** 50+ locations, R500M+ annual revenue, sophisticated operations (estimated 80-120 groups)

**Tier 2 - Mid-Size Networks:** 5-20 locations, R50M-500M annual revenue, growing operational complexity (estimated 300-500 groups)

**Tier 3 - Single/Dual Location:** 1-3 locations, R5M-50M annual revenue, lean operations (estimated 2,500-4,500 dealerships)

GrayArx targets all three segments with tiered pricing that scales with dealership size and complexity.

## Pricing Strategy Recommendations

### Market Entry (Year 1)

**Launch with Bundled Tiers as Primary Offering:**
- Bundled tiers are easier to communicate and compare
- Customers perceive greater value (20-50% discount vs. modular)
- Simpler sales process with fewer SKUs
- Reduces decision paralysis

**Promotional Pricing for Early Adopters:**
- First 100 customers: 30% discount on annual plans
- Referral bonus: 1 free month for each referred dealership
- Longer commitment incentive: 25% discount for 2-year contracts

### Scaling (Year 2-3)

**Introduce Modular Pricing for Custom Needs:**
- Customers wanting specific modules can purchase à la carte
- Enables upselling from Starter → Professional → Enterprise
- Supports niche use cases (e.g., service-only dealerships)

**Implement Usage-Based Pricing (Optional):**
- For large dealership groups: R 0.50 per vehicle per month (after 2,000 vehicles)
- Encourages adoption without artificial limits
- Aligns pricing with customer value
