## Contingency Planning

**If Dealership Needs to Reschedule:**
- Offer alternative dates within 2 weeks
- Maintain same 4-day structure
- Adjust support team schedule
- Update calendar and tracking

**If Support Team Member Unavailable:**
- Assign backup support person
- Brief backup on dealership status
- Maintain continuity of service
- Document handoff

**If Technical Issue Occurs:**
- Activate incident response procedures
- Provide immediate workaround
- Update dealership on status
- Extend onboarding if needed
