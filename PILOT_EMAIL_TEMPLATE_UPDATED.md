## Personalization Tips

1. **Use their name** (not "Hi there")
2. **Mention their dealership** (shows you researched them)
3. **Reference their location** ("other dealerships in Sandton")
4. **Use their pain point** ("I know lead capture is tough")
5. **Keep it short** (150-200 words max)

---

## What NOT to Do

❌ Don't mention pricing or tiers (we're gathering feedback first)
❌ Don't use generic "Hi there"
❌ Don't make it too long (keep under 200 words)
❌ Don't use too many emojis
❌ Don't make big promises ("guaranteed 100 leads")
❌ Don't ask for a call without showing value first
❌ Don't send from a generic email (use your name)
❌ Don't send at random times (send Tuesday-Thursday, 9am-11am)
❌ Don't pressure them to cancel existing systems
❌ Don't make it sound like a replacement (it's complementary)

---

## Timing

**Best days to send:** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
**Best times to send:** 9am-11am or 2pm-3pm
**Avoid:** Monday (busy), Friday (checking out), Weekends

## Pilot Feedback Collection

During the 30-day pilot, collect feedback on:

1. **What worked well?**
   - Which features did they love?
   - How many leads did they capture?
   - How much time did it save?

2. **What didn't work?**
   - Any issues or bugs?
   - Missing features?
   - Confusing parts?

3. **What would they pay for?**
   - What's the value they got?
   - What tier would fit their business?
   - What additional features would they want?

4. **Would they recommend it?**
   - Would they tell other dealerships?
   - What would make it better?
   - Any objections?

This feedback will directly inform the tiers and pricing we create after the pilot.

---

## Post-Pilot Strategy

After 30 days, we'll have real data from dealerships:
- How many leads they captured
- How much time they saved
- What features they used most
- What they'd pay for
- What they want next

**Then we'll create tiers that dealerships actually want**, not just what we think they need.

This is why we don't show pricing yet. We're gathering intelligence first.
