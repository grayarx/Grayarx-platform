## 1. Executive Summary

GrayArx should launch with three published tiers in **South African Rand**, priced **between local CRM tools and global DMS platforms**:

| Tier | Monthly (incl. VAT) | Annual (save ~17%) | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | **R 1 499 / month** | R 14 990 / year | Single-rooftop independents, up to 50 vehicles in stock |
| **Professional** | **R 3 499 / month** | R 34 990 / year | Growing independents and small franchises, up to 250 vehicles, all four AI agents fully on |
| **Enterprise** | **From R 7 999 / month** | Custom | Multi-rooftop groups, custom integrations, dedicated CSM |

**Recommended add-ons:** outbound call minutes (pass-through Twilio cost + 25 %), and a one-time onboarding/data-import fee of **R 4 999** waived for annual contracts. (All 11 SA official languages plus Portuguese are included in every tier — language is not a paywall.)

**Free 14-day trial** on Starter and Professional, no credit card required — this is critical in SA where dealers are price-sensitive and trust is earned slowly.

## 2. What the SA Market Currently Pays

The pricing range below was assembled from public price lists, dealer interviews quoted in industry press, and competitor comparison pages (Leadtrekker, BaseCloud, Cars.co.za, AutoTrader, Lightstone, Microsoft Dynamics SA, BiT DMS, AutoManager, DealerCenter).

### 2.1 Local SA tools (Rand-denominated, dealer-aware)

| Product | What it does | Published price (ZAR) |
|---|---|---|
| **Leadtrekker** (SA-built CRM) | Lead capture, assignment, basic automation | **R 220 / month** flat |
| **BaseCloud CRM** (SA-built) | CRM + WhatsApp + Sage/Xero + AI call summary | **R 850 / user / month**, –20 % annual |
| **Lightstone Auto — RiskMonitor on Signio** | Risk + valuations module bolt-on | **R 92 / month / dealership** |

| **Cars.co.za Premium Plus** | Marketing/listing visibility uplift | Not publicly listed; dealer-quoted ~R 4 000 – R 12 000 / month depending on stock size |
| **AutoTrader Dealer packages** | Listing + advertising + new AI tools | Not publicly listed; dealer-quoted ~R 5 000 – R 25 000+ / month |
| **Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales** | Generic enterprise CRM (used by some franchises) | **R 355 – R 30 226 / month** |

**Key takeaway:** the SA market splits into **two camps**:

- **Cheap & generic** (Leadtrekker, BaseCloud) — well under R 1 000/month, but not dealer-specialised and definitely no AI agents that call/email/book on the dealer's behalf.
- **Expensive & marketing-only** (Cars.co.za, AutoTrader) — R 5 000–R 25 000/month, but those are essentially classifieds packages, not operations software.

There is a clear **whitespace between R 1 500 and R 5 000 / month** for a product that actually *does the work* (drafts replies, calls prospects, books test drives, manages inventory) instead of just listing cars or tracking leads.

## 3. What GrayArx Should Charge — and Why

### 3.1 Starter — R 1 499 / month (R 14 990 / year)

**Target dealer:** independent used-car dealer in Joburg / Durban / Cape Town suburbs, 1 rooftop, 15–50 vehicles in stock, 1–3 sales staff. Typically the founder + a salesperson.

**Includes:**
- All 4 AI agents (Mia, Themba, Lerato, Sipho) **with a 200-action/month cap** across them combined
- Up to **50 active vehicle listings** with phone-camera photo upload
- Lead capture + Bookings + Inventory + Dealer Network gallery
- **All 11 SA official languages** active (no language limits on any tier)
- Email-only support, 1-business-day SLA
- POPIA-compliant data handling, owner notifications

**Why R 1 499?** It clears the psychological barrier of "under R 1 500", lands just above Leadtrekker (R 220) and BaseCloud's entry seat (R 850), but well below Cars.co.za's marketing packages. Dealers who try it for the free trial will compare it to *one salesperson's overtime cost for a single Saturday* (~R 600–R 900), which makes the maths easy.

### 3.2 Professional — R 3 499 / month (R 34 990 / year)  ← **most dealers will land here**

**Target dealer:** established independent or small franchise (Honda/Suzuki/Haval single-brand), 1–2 rooftops, 50–250 vehicles, 3–8 sales staff, has a marketing budget.

**Includes everything in Starter plus:**
- **Unlimited agent actions** (Mia drafts every reply, Sipho scouts daily, Themba calls qualified prospects automatically, Lerato confirms every booking)
- **Up to 250 active vehicle listings**
- **All 11 SA official languages plus Portuguese** active simultaneously
- **Nightly Prospector schedule** with weekly region rotation (auto-discovers prospects for you)
- **Outbound call integration** with R 500 / month of included call minutes (then pass-through Twilio cost + 25 %)
- WhatsApp Business handoff (manual for now, automated in v2)
- Custom email signature with your dealership logo + GrayArx co-brand
- Priority email + WhatsApp support, 4-hour business-day SLA
- Monthly performance report PDF

**Why R 3 499?** This is the **anchor price** — most pricing pages have a middle option that 60–70 % of customers choose, and the goal is to make Professional feel like the obvious value. It's:
- **Less than ⅓ the cost** of an AutoTrader Premium dealer package
- **About R 100 / day** — easily justifiable against the cost of one missed lead per week (average used-car gross margin in SA is R 18 000–R 35 000)
- Comparable to one part-time admin salary, but works 24/7 in all 11 SA official languages plus Portuguese

### 3.3 Enterprise — From R 7 999 / month (custom)

**Target dealer:** multi-rooftop group (Group1, Motus subsidiaries, larger independents like Auto Pedigree, NMI-DSM), 3+ branches, 250+ vehicles per branch.

**Includes everything in Professional plus:**
- **Unlimited vehicles, unlimited rooftops**
- **Per-rooftop agent personas** (so each branch has its own "Mia") with shared activity log
- DMS integrations (Tasima, Signio, Lightstone valuations, Hellopeter reviews)
- Custom roles & permissions (sales floor / F&I / management)
- Dedicated Customer Success Manager (monthly call)
- Signed Data Processing Agreement (POPIA Section 19 obligations explicit)
- 99.9 % uptime SLA with credits
- Custom onboarding & training (up to 8 hours included)

**Why "from R 7 999"?** Enterprise customers expect to be sold to, not to self-checkout. The "from" wording signals there is room for negotiation, lets you upsell per-rooftop, and ensures you never under-quote a multi-branch group that could happily pay R 20 000+ for a fully managed deployment.

## 5. Discounting Strategy (be disciplined)

- **Annual prepay:** standard 16.7 % discount (2 months free). Listed everywhere.
- **Quarterly prepay:** 5 % off. Only mention if asked.
- **Founding-50 launch promotion** (first 50 dealers signed in 2026): **lifetime 25 % off Professional** locked in. Creates urgency, gives you 50 case studies, and the lifetime lock-in turns them into evangelists. Cap it visibly — "23 of 50 spots left" — once you actually have data.
- **Referral credit:** any existing dealer who refers a paying signup gets **R 1 000 account credit**, capped at R 12 000 / year per dealer.
- **No ad-hoc discounting.** When a dealer pushes back on price, offer to extend the free trial to 30 days instead of cutting price — that protects your anchor.

## 6. Hidden Costs You Need to Cover With Each Sale

For the pricing above to actually be profitable, **make sure each Professional customer covers**:

| Cost item | Estimate per dealer per month |
|---|---|
| LLM (Mia drafting + Sipho prospecting + Themba scripts) | R 180 – R 400 |
| Twilio voice (Themba's outbound calls) | R 250 – R 800 (depends on call volume) |
| Email sending (SendGrid / Resend) | R 60 – R 150 |
| Hosting (Manus / Cloud Run / DB) | R 80 – R 150 per dealer (spread) |
| Storage (vehicle photos at ~50 MB / dealer) | R 20 – R 60 |
| Support time (assume 30 min / dealer / month) | R 150 – R 250 at junior-CSM rate |
| **Total variable cost** | **≈ R 740 – R 1 810 / dealer / month** |

At **R 3 499 / month**, gross margin per Professional dealer is **48 % – 79 %** — healthy for a SaaS at this stage. Starter at R 1 499 is **thinner** (margin 0–50 %) and should be considered a customer-acquisition tier, not a profit centre.

## 8. Sanity-Check: What's a Lead Actually Worth to a SA Dealer?

The whole pricing argument collapses if a dealer doesn't believe GrayArx pays for itself. Here's the working you should rehearse for every sales call:

- Average SA used-car gross margin per unit: **R 18 000 – R 35 000** (source: Lightstone Auto and industry surveys 2024–2025)
- Average dealer close rate on inbound leads: **8 – 12 %**
- That means **every inbound enquiry is worth R 1 600 – R 4 200** in expected gross margin
- If GrayArx recovers even **one extra closed deal per month** (by replying faster, not missing after-hours leads, or surfacing a prospect the dealer would have missed), it has covered its own subscription **and** added margin
- The Professional tier at R 3 499 pays for itself with **0.8 to 2.2 extra deals per year**

That's a low bar.

## 9. What I'd Test First (before locking these prices in)

1. **Run a small "founding 50" cohort at R 2 499 / month for Professional** for the first 90 days — log every objection. If <10 % of trials convert, the price is wrong. If >25 % convert, raise to R 3 499.
2. **Track the per-dealer LLM + Twilio spend weekly** for the first quarter. If Themba's calling costs blow past R 800/dealer/month, either rate-limit Twilio or move calls to an add-on.
3. **A/B test the pricing page**: half the visitors see R 1 499 / R 3 499 / R 7 999, half see R 1 799 / R 3 999 / R 8 999. Run for 30 days. Pick the variant with higher conversion × revenue.
4. **Don't list Enterprise pricing publicly until you have 3 signed Enterprise contracts.** It's better to negotiate from a position of "let's design the right package for you" than to anchor low.
