## Demo Link Strategy

**Option 1: Direct Link**
- Link to live demo: https://grayarx.manus.space/demo
- Shows real chatbot in action
- Dealership can test immediately

**Option 2: Calendly Link**
- Link to book a 15-minute call
- You personally show the demo
- Higher conversion (personal touch)

**Option 3: Video Link**
- 5-minute demo video (your recording)
- Dealership watches at their own pace
- Link to book call if interested

**Recommendation:** Use Option 2 (Calendly) for first 20 dealerships, then Option 1 (direct link) for scale.

---

## Key Metrics to Track

- **Open Rate:** Target 25-35%
- **Click Rate:** Target 5-10%
- **Demo Booking Rate:** Target 20-30% of clicks
- **Trial Signup Rate:** Target 50-70% of demos
- **Paid Conversion Rate:** Target 30-50% of trials

---

## Personalization Tips

1. **Use their name** (not "Hi there")
2. **Mention their dealership** (shows you researched them)
3. **Reference their location** ("other dealerships in Sandton")
4. **Use their pain point** ("I know lead capture is tough")
5. **Keep it short** (150-200 words max)

## A/B Testing Plan

**Week 1-2:** Test subject lines
- Send Version A to 25 dealerships
- Send Version B to 25 dealerships
- Send Version C to 25 dealerships
- Track open rates

**Week 3-4:** Test email body
- Send Version 1 to 25 dealerships (best subject line)
- Send Version 2 to 25 dealerships (best subject line)
- Send Version 3 to 25 dealerships (best subject line)
- Track click rates

**Week 5+:** Scale with best performing combo
- Use winning subject line + email body
- Send to remaining dealerships
- Optimize based on results

---

## What NOT to Do

❌ Don't use generic "Hi there"
❌ Don't make it too long (keep under 200 words)
❌ Don't use too many emojis
❌ Don't make big promises ("guaranteed 100 leads")
❌ Don't ask for a call without showing value first
❌ Don't send from a generic email (use your name)
❌ Don't send at random times (send Tuesday-Thursday, 9am-11am)

---

## Timing

**Best days to send:** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
**Best times to send:** 9am-11am or 2pm-3pm
**Avoid:** Monday (busy), Friday (checking out), Weekends
