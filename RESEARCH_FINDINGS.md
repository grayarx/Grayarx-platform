# Research Findings: South African Dealership Market & Pricing Models

## Key Market Insights

### Dealership Revenue Breakdown (Global Standard)
- **New Cars:** 58% of sales, but only 26% of gross profit
- **Used Cars:** 31% of sales, ~25% of gross profit
- **Service & Parts:** Only 11% of sales, but **49.6% of gross profit** ⭐ KEY INSIGHT
- **Finance & Insurance (F&I):** Significant additional revenue (90% of new car buyers, 73% of used car buyers)

### Critical Finding for GrayArx
**Dealerships make most money from SERVICE, not car sales.** This means:
- Subscription model alone won't capture full value
- Need to tie revenue to actual dealership success (leads → sales → service)
- Transaction-based revenue is more aligned with dealership profitability

## South African Market Context

### Dealership Software Pricing (Global Standard)
1. **User-Based Pricing:** $50-$200/user/month
   - Example: DMS with 10 users = $500-$2000/month
   - Per-user tiers (admin, sales, service)

2. **Transaction-Based Pricing:** 2-5% per transaction
   - F&I platforms: % per transaction
   - Parts ordering: Per-VIN charges
   - Service appointments: Per-booking fees

3. **Tiered Feature Pricing:** $300-$3000/month
   - Basic: $300-500/month (CRM + inventory)
   - Mid: $500-1000/month (+ marketing, reporting)
   - Enterprise: $1000-3000/month (+ AI, integrations)

4. **Hybrid Models:** Base subscription + usage/transaction fees
   - Most innovative approach
   - Aligns vendor success with customer success
   - Example: AutoFi (base fee + performance-based charges)

### South African Specific Factors
- Currency: ZAR (South African Rand)
- Average dealership size: 5-15 sales staff
- Tech adoption: Growing but cost-sensitive
- Preferred model: Hybrid (immediate + recurring revenue)

## Recommended Pricing Model for GrayArx

### Model: HYBRID TRANSACTION-BASED (Best for Dealerships)

**Why This Works:**
- ✅ Dealerships only pay when they succeed
- ✅ Aligns GrayArx incentives with dealership profitability
- ✅ Scales with dealership growth
- ✅ South African dealerships prefer this (low upfront risk)
- ✅ Higher lifetime value than subscription alone

### Proposed Structure (in ZAR)

**Tier 1: Starter** (1-5 sales staff)
- Monthly Base: R1,500
- Per Lead Generated: R50
- Per Test Drive Booked: R200
- Per Car Sold (if integrated): R500
- **Monthly Range:** R1,500 - R5,000+ (depending on activity)

**Tier 2: Professional** (6-15 sales staff)
- Monthly Base: R3,500
- Per Lead Generated: R40 (volume discount)
- Per Test Drive Booked: R150 (volume discount)
- Per Car Sold: R400 (volume discount)
- **Monthly Range:** R3,500 - R15,000+ (depending on activity)

**Tier 3: Enterprise** (15+ sales staff)
- Monthly Base: R7,500
- Per Lead Generated: R30 (volume discount)
- Per Test Drive Booked: R100 (volume discount)
- Per Car Sold: R300 (volume discount)
- Custom integrations available
- **Monthly Range:** R7,500 - R50,000+ (depending on activity)

### Financial Modeling

**Example: Mid-Size Dealership (10 sales staff)**

Baseline: R3,500/month subscription

**Conservative Month:**
- 20 leads generated: 20 × R40 = R800
- 5 test drives booked: 5 × R150 = R750
- Total: R3,500 + R800 + R750 = **R5,050/month**

**Average Month:**
- 50 leads generated: 50 × R40 = R2,000
- 15 test drives booked: 15 × R150 = R2,250
- 3 cars sold: 3 × R400 = R1,200
- Total: R3,500 + R2,000 + R2,250 + R1,200 = **R9,000/month**

**Strong Month:**
- 100 leads generated: 100 × R40 = R4,000
- 30 test drives booked: 30 × R150 = R4,500
- 8 cars sold: 8 × R400 = R3,200
- Total: R3,500 + R4,000 + R4,500 + R3,200 = **R15,200/month**

**Annual Revenue per Dealership:** R108,000 - R182,400/year

## Referral Program with Hybrid Model

### 20% Commission on What?

**Option A: Commission on Transaction Revenue Only**
- Dealership A refers B
- B generates 50 leads/month at R40 = R2,000
- A gets 20% of R2,000 = R400/month
- Year 1 total: R4,800

**Option B: Commission on Total Revenue (Recommended)**
- Dealership A refers B
- B pays R3,500 base + R2,000 transactions = R5,500/month
- A gets 20% of R5,500 = R1,100/month
- Year 1 total: R13,200

**Option C: Commission on Subscription Only (Conservative)**
- Dealership A refers B
- B pays R3,500 subscription
- A gets 20% of R3,500 = R700/month
- Year 1 total: R8,400

### Recommended: Option B (Total Revenue Share)

**Why:**
- ✅ Incentivizes quality referrals (successful dealerships)
- ✅ Rewards long-term relationships
- ✅ Aligns with dealership success
- ✅ Higher motivation for referrers
- ✅ Scales with both parties' growth

**Example Referral Earnings:**
- Refer 5 dealerships in Year 1
- Each averages R9,000/month
- 20% share = R1,800/month per referral
- Total from 5 referrals: R9,000/month recurring
- Year 1 total: R54,000 (5 × R1,800 × 6 months average)
- Year 2 total: R108,000 (full year)
