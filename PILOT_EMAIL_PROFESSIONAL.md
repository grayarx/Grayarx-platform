## Demo Call Script

### Opening (2 minutes)
"Thank you for taking the time. The purpose of this call is to give you a clear understanding of how GrayArx operates and whether it might be valuable for your dealership. This is a low-pressure conversation—my goal is simply to ensure you have the information you need to make an informed decision."

### Problem Statement (2 minutes)
"Most dealerships experience lead leakage in three areas: after-hours inquiries, WhatsApp messages, and customers who reach out but don't immediately connect with your team. These prospects often move to competitors within 24 hours. GrayArx is specifically designed to capture these lost opportunities."

### Solution Overview (3 minutes)
"GrayArx operates as an independent system. It doesn't replace or modify anything you're currently doing. Instead, it adds a new channel for lead capture and qualification. When a customer inquires through your website or WhatsApp, our system responds immediately, qualifies the lead, and delivers it to your sales team through your preferred channel—email, SMS, or CRM integration."

### Operational Impact (2 minutes)
"From an operational standpoint, GrayArx requires minimal setup. You provide your vehicle inventory, and our system learns it automatically. There's no staff retraining, no workflow disruption, and no integration headaches. Your team simply receives more qualified leads."

### Pilot Structure (2 minutes)
"We're offering a 30-day pilot at no cost. During this period, you'll have full visibility into lead volume, quality metrics, and conversion data. At the end of 30 days, you'll have concrete data to determine whether this is valuable for your business. There's no obligation to continue."

### Objection Handling

**"We already have a lead generation system."**
"That's great. GrayArx isn't a replacement—it's an addition. Most dealerships we work with already have existing systems. GrayArx simply captures leads through channels those systems might not be monitoring, particularly after-hours and WhatsApp inquiries."

**"How much does it cost?"**
"The pilot is completely free for 30 days. After the pilot, we'll discuss pricing based on your specific needs and the results you've seen. There's no commitment during the pilot period."

**"How long does setup take?"**
"Typically 15-20 minutes. You upload your vehicle inventory, our system learns it, and you're ready to start capturing leads. We can have you operational within an hour if needed."

**"Will this disrupt our current operations?"**
"No. GrayArx operates independently. Your existing systems, workflows, and team processes remain completely unchanged. We simply add an additional source of qualified leads."

**"What if we don't like it?"**
"After 30 days, if it's not working for your dealership, we simply discontinue. There's no penalty, no long-term commitment, and no complicated cancellation process. The pilot is designed to give you risk-free visibility into whether this is valuable for your business."

## Key Messaging Principles

**What to Emphasize:**
- Independent system (not a replacement)
- Seamless integration (no disruption)
- 24/7 lead capture (after-hours coverage)
- Qualified leads (pre-screened by AI)
- Measurable results (concrete data)
- Zero risk pilot (30 days, no cost)
- No long-term commitment

**What to Avoid:**
- Technical jargon
- Overpromising results
- Pressure tactics
- Pricing discussions (until after pilot)
- Comparisons to competitors
- Negative language about their current systems

## Tone & Voice Guidelines

- **Professional but approachable** - Business language without being stuffy
- **Confident but not arrogant** - We know what we're offering is valuable, but we respect their decision
- **Problem-focused** - Lead with their challenges, not our features
- **Results-oriented** - Emphasize measurable outcomes, not technical capabilities
- **Transparent** - Be clear about what we're doing and why
- **Respectful of their time** - Keep communications concise and valuable

GrayArx is not competing with:
- CRM systems (we integrate with them)
- Existing lead sources (we complement them)
- Sales teams (we support them)

GrayArx is solving:
- Lead leakage (after-hours, WhatsApp, missed inquiries)

## Pilot Cohort Management

**Target:** 100 dealerships across Gauteng
**Pilot cohort size:** 10-15 dealerships (to manage closely)
**Phased rollout:** 3-4 cohorts of 25-30 dealerships each
**Timeline:** Cohort 1 (Week 1-4), Cohort 2 (Week 5-8), etc.

This allows us to:
- Gather detailed feedback from each cohort
- Iterate and improve between cohorts
- Build case studies and testimonials
- Refine messaging and positioning
- Create data-driven tier structure
