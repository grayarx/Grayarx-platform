## **What is GrayArx?**

Imagine you own a toy store. When customers come in, you want to know:

- How do you know if they're really interested?

---

## **The 5 Main Parts of GrayArx**

### **1. 🤖 The Smart Chatbot (AI Assistant)**

**What it does**: Talks to customers 24/7, answers their questions, and learns what they want.

**How it works**:
- A customer visits your dealership website
- They chat with GrayArx (the AI chatbot)
- GrayArx asks smart questions like "What type of car interests you?" and "What's your budget?"
- GrayArx remembers everything the customer says
- The chatbot speaks in 7 different languages (English, Zulu, Xhosa, Sotho, Tswana, Afrikaans, Venda)

**Why it matters**: Your sales team doesn't have to answer the same questions 100 times. GrayArx does it automatically, 24 hours a day.

---

### **2. 📚 Self-Training FAQ System**

**What it does**: Automatically creates a list of common questions and answers based on what customers actually ask.

**How it works**:
- Every time a customer asks a question, GrayArx remembers it
- When the same question gets asked 3+ times, GrayArx automatically creates an FAQ entry
- The FAQ keeps getting better as more customers ask questions
- No manual work needed - it learns on its own

**Why it matters**: You don't have to manually write FAQs. GrayArx figures out what customers really want to know and answers them automatically.

---

### **3. 🎯 Lead Scoring System (Hot Lead Detection)**

**What it does**: Grades each customer like a school report card (A, B, C, D, F) to show which ones are most likely to buy.

**How it works**:
- GrayArx watches how customers behave:
  - How many times did they visit?
  - Did they book a test drive?
  - Did they apply for financing?
  - How interested do they seem?
  - How quickly do they respond?
- Based on these behaviors, GrayArx gives them a score (0-100)
- **Grade A** = "This person is very likely to buy!" (Hot Lead)
- **Grade F** = "This person is just browsing" (Cold Lead)

**Why it matters**: Your sales team can focus on the customers most likely to buy, instead of wasting time on people who aren't interested.

---

### **4. 📋 Lead Management & Booking System**

**What it does**: Automatically organizes customers and helps schedule test drives and financing appointments.

**How it works**:
- **Test Drive Booking**: Customers can book a test drive directly through GrayArx. It knows your dealership hours and available slots.
- **Financing Pre-Approval**: Customers can apply for financing. GrayArx collects their information and shows them what they might qualify for.
- **Trade-In Valuation**: Customers can enter their current car details, and GrayArx estimates how much it's worth.
- **Lead Assignment**: Your sales team gets notified immediately when a hot lead appears.

**Why it matters**: Everything is organized automatically. No more lost customer information or missed opportunities.

---

### **5. 📊 Admin Dashboard (The Control Center)**

**What it does**: Shows you everything happening with your dealership - like a dashboard in a car that shows speed, fuel, and temperature.

**How it works**:
- **Real-Time Metrics**: See how many leads you have, how many converted to sales, revenue, etc.
- **Lead Pipeline**: Watch customers move from "interested" → "test drive booked" → "financing applied" → "sold"
- **Chatbot Performance**: See which questions customers ask most, how satisfied they are
- **Sales Analytics**: Track which team members are closing the most deals
- **Custom Branding**: Upload your dealership logo and colors so everything looks like YOUR dealership

**Why it matters**: You can see everything at a glance. No more wondering "How are we doing today?"
