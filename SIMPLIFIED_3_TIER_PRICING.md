## Pricing Justification

### Why These Price Points?

**TIER 1 (R 1,999):**
- Competitive with BluWave CRM (R 1,860-3,100 for 3-5 users)
- Competitive with DealerCenter CRM Plus ($99 ~R 1,700)
- **Advantage:** No per-user fees, includes SMS/email automation
- **Target:** Price-sensitive SMBs, service-focused dealerships

**TIER 2 (R 4,999):**
- 2.5x Tier 1 price, but includes 5 major features (WhatsApp, booking, advanced docs, reporting, multi-location)
- Competitive with DealerCenter CRM Pro + DMS ($99+$199 = $298 ~R 5,100)
- **Advantage:** Single platform, no integration needed, includes AI agents
- **Target:** Growing networks, full dealership operations

**TIER 3 (R 9,999):**
- 2x Tier 2 price, but includes calling agent + unlimited locations + integrations
- Competitive with enterprise DMS solutions (R 10,000-20,000/month)
- **Advantage:** All-in-one platform, no need for multiple tools
- **Target:** Large groups, franchise networks, enterprise customers

**Key Insight:** All tiers deliver exceptional ROI (427-885% annually), making the upgrade decision obvious for dealerships.
