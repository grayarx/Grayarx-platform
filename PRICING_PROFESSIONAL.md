# GrayArx Pricing & Value Proposition

## Executive Summary

GrayArx is a specialized lead generation and customer engagement platform designed to operate as an independent revenue channel within dealership operations. The platform captures, qualifies, and delivers customer inquiries across multiple touchpoints without requiring modifications to existing dealership infrastructure.

---

## Pricing Structure

GrayArx operates on a tiered subscription model, with pricing determined by dealership scale and operational requirements.

### Starter Tier - R3,999/Month

**Ideal for:** Independent dealerships and small multi-location operations

**Included:**
- 24/7 AI-powered customer engagement (website + WhatsApp)
- Automated lead qualification and scoring
- Multi-language support (8+ languages)
- Lead capture and storage (unlimited)
- Basic analytics dashboard
- Email notifications for new leads
- CSV export functionality
- Email support (24-48 hour response)

**Expected Performance:**
- 35-50 qualified leads per month
- Lead-to-test-drive conversion: 25-35%
- Average response time: <2 minutes

---

### Professional Tier - R7,999/Month

**Ideal for:** Multi-location dealerships and high-volume operations

**Included (Everything in Starter, plus):**
- Advanced lead scoring and prioritization
- Webhook integration support (real-time lead delivery)
- CRM integration guidance
- API access for custom integrations
- Advanced analytics and reporting
- Lead performance metrics
- Dedicated onboarding support
- Priority email support (12-24 hour response)
- Weekly performance reviews (first month)

**Expected Performance:**
- 50-75 qualified leads per month
- Lead-to-test-drive conversion: 30-40%
- Webhook delivery reliability: 99.5%+

---

### Enterprise Tier - R11,999/Month

**Ideal for:** Large dealership groups and premium operations

**Included (Everything in Professional, plus):**
- Full API access with custom development support
- Dedicated technical account manager
- Real-time webhook management and optimization
- Custom lead scoring models (trained on dealership data)
- Advanced CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive)
- Priority phone support (4-8 hour response)
- Bi-weekly business reviews
- Custom reporting and dashboards
- Slack channel access for support
- Quarterly strategy sessions

**Expected Performance:**
- 75-100+ qualified leads per month
- Lead-to-test-drive conversion: 35-45%
- Dedicated infrastructure and optimization

## Justification for Pricing

### Why R3,999 for Starter?

**Competitive Analysis:**
- Market average for lead generation: R4,500-6,000/month
- GrayArx Starter: R3,999 (15-30% below market)
- Positioning: Premium features at accessible price

**Value Delivered:**
- 35-50 leads/month × R8,000 average = R280,000-400,000 revenue potential
- Cost: R3,999 (0.99%-1.4% of revenue potential)
- ROI: 7,000% to 10,000%

**Psychological Pricing:**
- R3,999 (just under R4,000) feels more accessible than R4,000
- Suggests premium positioning without premium price
- Easier to justify to dealership management

### Why R7,999 for Professional?

**Value Addition:**
- Starter features + API + Webhooks + CRM integration
- 50-75 leads/month (50% increase from Starter)
- Real-time lead delivery (vs. email notifications)
- Dedicated support

**Justification:**
- R7,999 = 2× Starter price
- But value delivered = 3-4× (additional features + lead volume)
- Professional tier margin: 75.2% (vs. Starter 50.4%)
- Targets dealerships with higher revenue potential

### Why R11,999 for Enterprise?

**Premium Positioning:**
- Full API + custom integrations + dedicated account manager
- 75-100+ leads/month (100% increase from Starter)
- Quarterly business reviews and strategy sessions
- Highest support level

**Justification:**
- R11,999 = 3× Starter price
- But value delivered = 5-6× (enterprise features + lead volume + support)
- Enterprise tier margin: 83.4% (highest profitability)
- Targets dealership groups and premium operations
