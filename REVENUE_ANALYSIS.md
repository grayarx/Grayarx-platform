## Monthly Revenue Calculation (Professional Tier Dealership)

### Scenario: Average Dealership with 8 Sales Staff

**Base Subscription**: R3,500/month

**Variable Charges**:
- **Leads generated**: 40 leads/month × R40 = **R1,600**
- **Test drive bookings**: 12 bookings/month × R150 = **R1,800**
- **New car sales**: 5 sales/month × R1,000 = **R5,000**
- **Used car sales**: 7 sales/month × R500 = **R3,500**

**Total Variable Charges**: R1,600 + R1,800 + R5,000 + R3,500 = **R11,900**

**Monthly Total**: R3,500 + R11,900 = **R15,400**
